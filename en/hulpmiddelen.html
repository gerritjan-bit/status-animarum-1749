Estimate the parents’ year of birth

A woman almost always had her children between the ages of 18 and 40. If you know when her children were born or baptised, you can work out roughly when she herself was born. Enter the year of birth of the oldest and the youngest child you know of.

This is a rule of thumb, not a certainty. Some women still had a child after 40, up to about 45; adjust the limit if needed. If you do not know the first or last child, the limits still hold, but the range becomes wider than necessary. The more years between the oldest and the youngest child, the more precise the estimate.
