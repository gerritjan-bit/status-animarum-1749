What it contains

The index contains the surnames, first names and roles of all persons in the baptism, marriage and burial registers of 36 parishes within about 27 km of Vreden and Stadtlohn, for the years 1750–1820. These are the Catholic registers that the Bistumsarchiv Münster makes available on Matricula Online.

How it was read

Every scan was read by an AI model (Claude Haiku), which listed the persons in each record with their role. Double pages were read as a left and a right half. A sample checked against own transcriptions gave about 90% correctly read names in clear handwriting (Vreden) and about 70–85% in difficult handwriting (Stadtlohn). So there are errors: misread names, swapped roles, wrong years. Always check the scan.

Spelling groups and links

The same name often appears in ten different spellings (Lütkenhues, Lütkenhaus, Lutkenhuis …). The search groups such spellings together, using Westphalian spelling rules and the sound of the name (Kölner Phonetik). This is a suggestion by the computer, not a fact: sometimes two different names are grouped. Every search shows which groups are included, and you can switch them on or off.

Where the source itself gives a second name (nunc, dicta, genannt, geb.), this is shown as a link. That link, too, was read by the computer.

What is missing

Registers have not survived for every parish and year. Known gaps include: Vreden (baptisms before 1764, burials before 1777), Stadtlohn (baptisms 1808–1820), Coesfeld St. Lamberti (baptisms and marriages 1752–1762), Zwillbrock (marriages and burials before 1811), Burlo (burials only), Gronau (before c. 1767) and, in many parishes, burials up to c. 1763–1770.
